# Exceptions

Section 1.5 of `docs/DEV-STANDARD.md`. Each exception has an owner and an expiry date; an expired exception is either fixed or approved again.

| # | Section | Exception | Reason | Risk | Compensating controls | Owner | Approved | Expires |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8.5.3 | The two testimonial videos have no captions. | The videos arrived without a transcript, and captions cannot be written reliably without one. | Deaf and hard-of-hearing visitors miss the spoken content of two videos. | The player is keyboard operable and never plays on its own; each video has an accessible name; the dashboard has a captions field per video (a `.vtt` file from the media library) that adds the track the moment a file is set; the accessibility statement lists the gap. | Multi Digital owner | 2026-11-23 (confirmed by the owner) | 2026-12-31 |
| 2 | 8.4.6 | The eleven testimonials are screenshots of messages: text inside images. | The design shows the original WhatsApp and email messages as proof; retyping them would change what they are. | Screen-reader users get a general description, not the wording of each message. | Every image has alt text (editable in the dashboard); the section has its own heading; the accessibility statement lists the gap. A transcript per image can be added as alt text once the client supplies the wording. | Multi Digital owner | 2026-11-23 (confirmed by the owner) | 2027-03-31 |
